Glossary

Key terms with the Korean wording used on the original slides, so you can recognize them in Korean exam questions.

The original decks mix Korean and English. Many technical terms appear only in English even in the Korean slides — for example Intrinsic, Extrinsic, Focal Length, Principal Point, Homography, Reprojection Error, Baseline, Disparity, Epipolar Line, Rectification, Time Offset, Rolling Shutter, Odometry, Encoder, Quaternion, Kalman gain, Innovation, Bias, Landmark, Jacobian, Sigma point, Loop closure, Pose, Hessian, Line search, Armijo, Levenberg–Marquardt, KKT, QP and SQP. The table lists the terms that the slides write in Korean.
