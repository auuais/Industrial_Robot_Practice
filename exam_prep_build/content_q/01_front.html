How to use this question bank

Ten questions per lecture, each answerable in a few sentences and a sketch. None of them needs a calculator.

These questions check whether you understand why the methods work and what happens when an assumption breaks — the kind of question that separates memorizing a formula from understanding it. A good answer names the mechanism, gives the reason, and, where useful, a small example or picture.

Conceptexplain an idea in your own words Analyzepredict, compare, diagnose or design

Dots show difficulty, from one (warm-up) to three (exam-hard). Answer out loud or on paper before reading the model answer, then compare: did you name the same mechanism? Chapter numbers match the lecture numbers and the chapters of the Exam Prep Notes.
