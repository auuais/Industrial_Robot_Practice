How to use these notes

One chapter that follows the 17 slides of Lecture 10 in order. Section numbers are 10.x, so they slot in after the Lecture 9 notes.

Every section starts with an In plain English box that explains the idea without formulas. Read it first, then the details and the worked examples. Examples labelled with a slide number work the lecture's own formulas through with numbers; Extra example boxes go further, often with a simulation of the controller. The chapter ends with a table comparing the controllers, a formula sheet and 18 practice problems with full solutions.
