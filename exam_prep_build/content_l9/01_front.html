How to use these notes

One chapter that follows the 24 slides of Lecture 9 in order. Section numbers are 9.x, so they slot in after the Lecture 8 notes.

Every section starts with an In plain English box that explains the idea without formulas. Read it first, then the details and the worked examples. Examples labelled with a slide number are the lecture's own examples worked out in full; Extra example boxes are new ones for practice. The chapter ends with a table comparing the ways to generate candidates, a formula sheet and 17 practice problems with full solutions.

The slides give the formulas but few numbers. To work the formulas through, these notes use one running example: a left curve of radius 20 m. The speed plans on slide 10 are not given as formulas; the plotted values match speed-keeping quartics (5 m/s slowing to 3 m/s or speeding up to 7 m/s over 4 s), so those are used and marked where they appear. The cost weights, scales and reference speed in the selection example are chosen for illustration. Every number was checked with code.
